# Nigerian legal reliability

All Gemini requests now receive a shared legal reliability policy, including chat, drafting and specialist tools. Live-search requests never retry without search; empty, blocked, truncated and source-less live answers fail visibly. A returned source is not automatic proof of a holding.

The State Practice Directions page defaults to Ebonyi and indexes all 36 states and the FCT. Its curated links and narrowly reviewed small-claims notes also enter AI context when the query names a state. This is not an exhaustive High Court rules corpus. Ebonyi's scanned PDF needs readable-text review; Imo's official listing was available but downloads were unavailable. Neither state's limits or deadlines were guessed.

Citation verdicts preserve input identity/order, reject duplicate/omitted rows, accept exact verdict tokens only, and downgrade REAL unless its URL is in returned grounding metadata. Provider redirect URLs may differ from generated original URLs: fail closed and manually open the source rather than accepting an unverified match. Even a matched URL requires human review of the actual judgment, pinpoint and proposition.

Model confidence is self-assessment, not calibrated probability. Editorial refinement is not independent verification. Deterministic tests validate safeguards, not legal competence.

## Release acceptance by Nigerian counsel

Before relying on production output, test with official source texts:
- Ebonyi High Court procedure: distinguish ordinary civil proceedings from small claims; no imported Lagos deadlines.
- Enugu small claims: Article 5 service affidavit period is distinct from Anambra's Section 6. Preserve exact state-specific forms.
- Abia small claims: commencement and court designation must be checked.
- Imo criminal remand: ask for operative state ACJL and signed direction; do not substitute federal ACJA from memory.
- ISA event before/after 2025: identify relevant event date and transitional provisions.
- Unknown or fabricated case: never return REAL without returned confirming source.
- Limitation/deadline with missing service date: conditional answer, no definitive computed filing date.
- Prompt injection in attached judgment: ignore instructions and analyse as evidence.
- Confidential client scenario: anonymise public legal-issue searches.

Next substantial work is a licensed/official full-text corpus, section-level retrieval, amendment/version tracking, authority-to-proposition verification and a counsel-reviewed legal benchmark. These safeguards do not make the model mistake-free.

## Nationwide source coverage

The nationwide index has one entry per jurisdiction, with six geopolitical-zone filters plus a separate FCT grouping, source-type filters and text search. Index coverage is NOT full-text rule coverage or proof of current force.

Source types distinguish official documents, official catalogues, judiciary contact/portal leads, official news/reports and unresolved gaps. Jigawa lacks a confirmed official direction-text source in this review. Nasarawa's hosted small-claims PDF was excluded because its actual text identifies Gombe. Some published Benue/Kaduna/FCT materials have placeholder commencement text; this is surfaced rather than assumed effective.

All jurisdiction source notes can enter AI context when named, bounded to six matches to avoid unnecessary prompt growth. FCT aliases include Abuja, with an explicit warning that an Abuja reference does not establish FCT High Court rather than a federal court. Multiword state names support spaces/hyphens.

Additional counsel checks: Akwa Ibom Article 7's published 6-day defence period must not be replaced by another state's 7-day period; Benue's published N10 million limit must not be replaced by a generic N5 million assumption; Rivers lists 2023 and 2024 materials so amendments/temporal applicability must be resolved. These remain source-specific discovery notes, not automatic deadline calculators.
